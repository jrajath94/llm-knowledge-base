Real-Time vs Batch Inference

System Design · Expert · Week 13

offline and online features can differ even without explicit bugs if they are computed at different as-of times.

The economics lab. On a simulated world where risk changes over time, you implement two scoring policies: score immediately when a spike event happens, or score in batches on a schedule. Then you measure freshness against cost. This is the latency-cost-freshness triangle made concrete: real-time scoring catches spikes faster but costs more per decision, batch scoring is cheap but stale. Watch for the policy-comparison discipline: same workload, same metrics, then decide. That discipline is exactly how you answer "real-time or batch" in an interview.
