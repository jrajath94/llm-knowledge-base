Feature Store Patterns

System Design · Expert · Week 13

The consistency lab. You build a synthetic event log, compute point-in-time correct offline features, then deliberately build leaky features to watch the evaluation metric inflate. The inflation is the lesson: without point-in-time correctness, your offline numbers are fiction. Then you simulate the online store and run the skew check that compares what training saw against what serving would see. Watch for the habit this installs: never trust an offline metric until you can prove the features were computed without peeking at the future.
